How I approach quality

The habits I bring to testing and collaboration.

01

Start with the user’s goal.

Understand the workflow, business rules, and the consequences of a failure before deciding what to test.

02

Look between the features.

Follow navigation, data, and state changes. A screen can work while the journey around it breaks.

03

Make the evidence useful.

Document clear steps and expected behavior so developers can investigate without guessing.

04

Close the loop.

Retest changes, revisit surrounding workflows, and communicate the risks that still need attention.

through a CRM migration.

Tested ReloSpec’s move to V3, comparing behavior across versions and checking how individual modules worked together.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Created the V3 test plan, reported reproducible defects, and followed fixes through retesting and broader regression.

Explore the case study +

The challenge

A migration can preserve individual screens while changing behavior between them. I focused on complete workflows as well as isolated features.

What I investigated

Filters resetting on navigation, duplicate checklist records, contact creation, sorting, and form data handling.

My approach

Compared earlier-version behavior with V3, mapped test coverage, and documented expected and actual results so the team could investigate and retest changes.

Why it mattered

The findings gave the team concrete evidence of regressions and made unresolved migration risks easier to discuss.

The challenge

A form can look correct while losing data or producing an unexpected downstream result.

My contribution

Investigated lead routing, scrolling and layout issues, form field handling, search behavior, branding settings, and SEO-related changes. Compared stage and production behavior where appropriate and worked with developers to validate fixes.

Why it mattered

Connecting the visible interaction to the resulting data helped uncover issues that a page-only review could miss.

The challenge

Document updates affect both the content people read and the fields they complete. Each needs to remain consistent with the intended workflow.

My contribution

What I learned
