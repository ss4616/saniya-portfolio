CRM PLATFORM / MIGRATION TESTING

Keeping critical workflows intact through a migration.

On ReloSpec’s move to V3, I tested individual modules and the wider system to check that everyday tasks still worked as expected.

My contribution
Created a V3 test plan, compared behavior across versions, reported reproducible defects, and retested fixes.
What I investigated
Filter persistence, duplicate records, contact creation, sorting, and form data handling.
Read the project notes +

The challenge

A migration can preserve individual screens while changing the behavior users rely on between them. I focused on complete workflows as well as isolated features.

How I approached it

I mapped module coverage, compared V2 and V3 behavior, documented steps and expected results, and followed fixes through retesting and broader regression.

Why it matters

This work gave the team concrete evidence about regressions and helped make remaining migration risks visible before release.

REAL ESTATE / WEB QUALITY

Testing the journey beyond the page.

Across Howard Hanna and Allen Tate web work, I validated customer interactions and investigated the behavior behind them.

My contribution
Tested modal changes, contact forms, agent and office pages, branding settings, and migration updates.
What I investigated
Scrolling and layout issues, lead routing, form field handling, search behavior, and SEO-related changes.
Read the project notes +

The challenge

A form can look correct while sending incomplete data or producing an unexpected downstream result. Visual checks are only one part of the journey.

How I approached it

I reproduced issues across relevant pages and states, compared stage and production behavior where appropriate, and collaborated with developers to validate fixes.

Why it matters

Connecting interface behavior to the resulting data helped the team investigate issues that a page-only check could miss.

02 / HOW I WORK

Curious by nature.
Structured by practice.

I turn “something feels off” into a clear finding a team can act on.

01

Understand the risk.

Start with the user’s goal, the business rules, and the workflows most affected by a change.

02

Follow the edges.

Explore alternate paths, state changes, and the places where one feature hands off to another.

03

Make it actionable.

Document reproducible steps, expected behavior, and useful evidence so the team can move forward.

04

Close the loop.

Retest the fix, check the surrounding behavior, and communicate what still needs attention.
